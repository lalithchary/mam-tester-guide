Before you begin

This is an early MVP, not production-ready. Do not enter real personal details, sensitive documents, banking information or actual apartment finances. Features and data may change or be lost.

Two roles, one shared picture.

The experience changes with your role. Please report any action or information that appears accessible to the wrong person.

01 / Caretaker

Manage the community

Sets up apartment information, approves residents, manages flats and monthly inputs, publishes bills, records payments and expenses, manages documents and can initiate a caretaker transfer.

Try: approve a fictional resident and check that a second approval or transfer behaves as expected.

02 / Resident

Follow your community

Registers and waits for approval, checks shared information, views bills and fund activity, participates in discussions, views documents and manages their own profile and private details.

Try: compare an unapproved account with an approved one, and check that caretaker-only actions are blocked.

Owner and tenant describe a resident’s relationship to a flat; they are not separate administrator roles.

Explore the whole workflow.

Use the month selector where available. The items below describe the current MVP, not guarantees of financial or security correctness.

01 Accounts

Sign in, register & manage your profile

Residents can request an account and wait for caretaker approval. Signed-in users can update their profile and change their password. The account menu also includes personal private-detail fields.

Test: sign in and out, try an incorrect password, edit fictional details, and check what a pending account can access.

02 Community

Flats, residents & caretaker transfer

The caretaker manages flat numbers, floors and billable status, reviews residents and associates people with flats as owner or tenant. A transfer is requested by the current caretaker and accepted by the intended successor.

Test: add fictional flats, review approval and confirm a transfer is not complete before acceptance.

03 Monthly inputs

Expenses, tankers & meter readings

Record shared expenses by category, tanker delivery dates, litres and costs, and per-flat previous and current meter readings. Expenses and tankers can be marked paid; expense receipt images are supported.

Test: try a lower current reading, a zero or invalid amount, an unpaid item and a fictional receipt image.

04 Statements

Preview and publish maintenance

The monthly calculation shares common expenses among billable flats, calculates tanker water charges from consumption and suggests a corpus amount that rounds maintenance to the next ₹100. A caretaker can override corpus and publish a statement, locking the month’s draft inputs.

Test: check the preview against a hand calculation, change a corpus amount and try editing after publication. Use imaginary numbers only.

05 Funds

Payments, ledger & corpus

Record fictional maintenance and corpus payments with a method such as cash, UPI or bank transfer. The shared ledger shows fund credits and debits, including paid expenses and tankers. A corpus withdrawal includes a reason and supporting document.

Test: compare a test payment or paid expense with the ledger, and try duplicate submissions. Do not initiate real payments.

06 Insights

Dashboard, charts & exports

The dashboard brings together monthly and fund information. Charts include 12-month water and average maintenance views plus a selectable flat water chart. A CSV report provides an export of statement data.

Test: change the month or flat, compare chart values with your fictional entries and inspect the exported CSV.

07 Neighbourhood

Discussions, documents & contacts

Residents can read and participate in discussion topics and replies. The app includes a shared document repository and watchman contact information maintained in apartment settings.

Test: post a harmless test message and open a fictional document. Shared content can be visible to other approved residents.

08 Everyday use

Mobile, theme & installability

The interface adapts to smaller screens and includes light/dark theme controls. A web app manifest and static cache are present; installation and offline behaviour depend on the browser and do not replace a working network connection.

Test: open on a phone and desktop, switch theme and report anything clipped, confusing or inaccessible.

Test carefully. Trust cautiously.

Role checks and password-protected accounts exist, but this is a prototype and has not completed a production security or accounting review.

What is in place

  • Caretaker-only checks on management actions and approval.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes; sessions expire.
  • Private-detail fields are restricted by the application to their owner.
  • Setup requires a key in production configuration.

What is not ready

  • No email verification, password-reset emails or comprehensive rate limiting.
  • Private details are stored unencrypted in SQLite; database and backup access can expose them.
  • No complete accounting reconciliation, revision/reversal workflow or automated backup guarantee.
  • Approved residents can see other approved residents’ phone numbers. Shared documents are not personal vaults.

Never post secrets in feedback. GitHub issues are public. Remove names, phone numbers, addresses, screenshots containing private data and account credentials.
